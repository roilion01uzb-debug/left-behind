# Left Behind

A Minecraft horror mod for **Fabric, Minecraft 26.3**. This is *Left Behind 1*.

Original Steve is always around. He isn't a monster. He watches you, learns from you, talks to you and remembers you.
Herobrine is always one step ahead of both of you, and he knows exactly which memories to wake up.

## Download

`downloads/left-behind-0.7.0.jar`. Put it in your `mods` folder together with Fabric API.

## What's in it

### A story that moves forward

The world goes through four acts, each announced with a title card: **The One Who Stayed**, **Familiar Things**,
**The Observer** and **What Is Left**. The story moves on when you have spent time with Steve, talked to him,
helped him remember and lived through his memory breaches. It doesn't move on by itself just because days pass.

- **Steve's Journal**: Steve hands you a book the first time you talk. Use it (or `/steve journal`) to read everything
  the world has given up so far: 18 memories, 8 torn pages and a set of survival notes.
- **Torn pages**: pieces of an old diary. They are found inside relics, dropped by ghosts and left where Herobrine stood.
- **Relics**: things nobody built start appearing near you. A tree with no leaves and a sign. A starting house with a chest.
  A brick pyramid. Something at 0, 0. Bring Steve near one and he remembers.
- **25 advancements** in their own "Left Behind" tab track all of it.

### A Steve who talks (new in 0.3)

Steve speaks English, and you talk to him the way you talk to anyone: **type in chat**. No menus, no buttons.

- **You can hear him (new in 0.4).** Around 700 of his lines are recorded with a natural voice and real emotion:
  calm, warm, curious, sad, hushed at night, scared, panicking and angry. He takes a breath before he says something
  frightening and sighs before something sad. The voice comes from where he stands, and his words type out at the
  pace he says them. Herobrine whispers in your head, in Steve's own voice gone wrong.
  The volume is the game's **Voice/Speech** slider. Lines built from what you tell him (your answers, words you teach him)
  are text only, and the recordings never say your name.

- **His words appear above his head**, typed out as he says them, and as a **subtitle just above your hotbar**
  when he is behind you or too far to read. Herobrine's whispers show up as grey subtitles.
- **Just type near him.** Within about 10 blocks he hears you if he can see you; once you are talking, 20 blocks is fine;
  say "Steve" and he hears you from 40. Slang and typos are fine ("u ok?", "wru doing", "helloooo").
- **He starts conversations himself.** He says hello when you come back, asks your name the first time you meet,
  and later asks you things: what you are afraid of, what you like to build, whether you dream, why you stay.
  He **remembers your answers** and brings them up again days later.
- **He learns words.** Mention something he has never heard of ("have you seen netherite?") and he asks what it is.
  Tell him, and from then on he knows it and remembers who taught him.
- **Ask him anything**: who he is, if he is real, about "him", about "the others", about this place, how he feels,
  what he remembers, what he thinks of you. Ask for a joke or a story. Over 60 subjects, and his answers change as the
  acts move on and as he trusts you more.
- **Ask him to do things**: "follow me", "stay here", "go where you want". He only follows people he trusts.
- **Hold something and right-click him** and he reacts to it: food, tools, flowers, music discs, a compass, bricks,
  a bed, TNT and about thirty other things. Gifts raise his trust. Right-clicking him with an empty hand gets his attention.
- **He comments on what you do**: mining, building, fighting, dying, sleeping, sneaking behind him, standing still too long,
  being on fire, the weather, the time of day. He keeps quiet about it while you are talking.
- Insult him and he remembers. Tell him to shut up and he will, for a while. Some things only "sorry" fixes.
- He still learns from you (chop enough trees and he starts chopping), still gets angry in stages if you hurt him,
  and defends players he trusts from monsters.
- Players without the mod on a server still get his words in chat.

### What's new in 0.7

- **Steve gets around like a player.** He swims (head up, kicking, diving after you), climbs ladders and vines, opens
  trapdoors over his head, opens and closes fence gates and doors, and when there is no path he makes one: he pillars
  up under himself, digs stairs down (never straight down), clears a two-high tunnel and bridges gaps crouching at the
  edge. He won't dig through bedrock, lava, water, or anybody's walls.
- **He watches out for himself.** He notices flowing water that would carry him over an edge, drops, lava, fire, a
  hissing creeper and running out of air, and gets clear before it happens. He eats when he's hurt and heals slowly.
- **No more crafting tables everywhere.** He carries his own table and furnace, puts them down when he needs them, and
  picks them back up when you move on.
- **He uses the world.** Chests (he stores what he doesn't need and doesn't take yours), ripe crops (harvests and
  replants), signs (reads them out), beds (sleeps when you sleep), note blocks (plays a tune he half remembers), bells,
  levers, buttons, doors, barrels, cakes, furnaces, lecterns, jukeboxes, flowers (picks one for you), and has something to
  say about nearly every other block.
- **He builds.** Say "build us a shelter" or "we need a real base": he finds flat ground, levels it, and builds walls,
  windows, a roof, a door facing you and torches inside, with the same blocks you build with. When it's getting dark and
  you have nowhere to go, he offers.
- **He understands more.** Reminders ("remind me to..."), plans ("what should we do?", "first we need a real base"), and
  when he doesn't get something he says what he can do instead of "say it simpler". "Follow me" gets him moving at once
  (he used to stand there talking for twenty seconds), and telling him to do something is never mistaken for the answer
  to a question he just asked you.
- **The story explains itself.** Every time something happens (a glimpse, the pillars, a ghost, a lost night) it is
  written down with the day, and the next time Steve is near you and calm he tells you what it was. The journal opens on
  **Where things stand**: the act you're in and what it means, how Steve is, what has happened lately, and a checklist of
  what moves the story on. When an act begins, chat says what it means.
- **New things happen to you**, one at a time with long gaps: your footsteps one step behind you, everything you built
  flickering out of the world for a second, Steve's voice saying old things from the dark, another Steve standing far
  away, every animal staring at the same empty spot, a sign with your own words on it, a few seconds of lost time, and
  your door opening by itself.
- **Sounds from the story instead of the usual horror drone**: Steve's tune on a far note block, the same tune played
  back wrong, a music box running down, knocks counting to seven and then eight, someone placing blocks or digging
  straight down out of sight, Steve humming, the tones of an old seed, the world resetting, a door opening slowly.
  27 new sound files, a new "presence" sound, and 184 new voiced lines for Steve.

### What's new in 0.6

- **Steve does his part.** He plays like a player now instead of carrying a wooden axe around. He chops trees, makes
  planks, a crafting table, sticks and a wooden pickaxe, digs stone for stone tools, finds iron, builds and lights a
  furnace, smelts, makes an iron pickaxe, mines coal and lights the dark around you at night, and when he finds something
  rare (diamonds, gold, emeralds, lapis, redstone) he brings it to you. He crafts with the game's real recipes, and his
  pockets are saved (he drops them if he dies). When he has nothing left to do he asks what you need.
- **He learns from what you make.** Craft something new where he can see, and he learns the recipe: "Ladder? I watched
  how you made that. Do you want one?" Say yes and he makes it himself, from the first log, table included. Things you
  keep making, he offers to make for you.
- **He understands sentences.** Ask in your own words: "can you get me some iron", "make me a chest", "do you have
  coal?", "how do I make a furnace", "where can I find diamonds" (he looks around you and tells you which way and how
  far), "what are you doing", "what do you have", "light it up", "stop working", "never mind", "I like emeralds" (he
  keeps some for you). Any item in the game by its name or a plain word for it (wood, stone, iron), with numbers. When
  he only gets part of it, he asks about that part instead of saying he doesn't understand.
- **He is in the moment.** He warns you about the creeper behind you, the skeleton, lava near your feet, drowning, falling,
  being hungry, digging straight down, walking around at night without armour, and thunder.
- **Herobrine from the first evening.** On day one, as the sun goes down, someone is standing far off at the edge of
  what you can see, facing you. Look properly and he isn't there. After that he is around now and then from day two.
- **Herobrine learns you.** He remembers the places you keep going back to (saved with the world) and which way you
  walk, and goes to stand where you're about to be. You get there and he has just left: "you always come this way".
  When he watches you, he comes a few blocks closer whenever your back is turned. His whispers come from what he knows
  about you: your house, your bed, how much Steve trusts you, what you're holding, where you dig.
- **Three structures** show up near you over the first days. **The Pillars**: seven stone pillars in a ring with a sign,
  "count them"; stand in the middle and look away, and there are eight. **The Hallway**: stone stairs going down out of the
  grass into a long hallway with words on the walls, ending in a small room someone lived in on their first night; when
  you reach it, the lights behind you go out one by one. **The Obsidian Box**: a closed cube of obsidian. Something inside
  knocks when you come close.
- **313 new sounds**: 260 new voiced lines (210 for Steve, 50 for Herobrine) and 53 new sound effects (Herobrine's
  footsteps, torches going out, stone grinding, distant footsteps, someone mining far away, cave drips, creaking wood,
  far doors, more knocks, breathing and wind).

### What's new in 0.5

- **You can catch Herobrine with Steve.** At dusk Steve may walk up somewhere high, a long way off, and talk to someone
  standing next to him. You only ever hear Steve. Get close, or stare, and there is only Steve: "Who? There's nobody."
  From Act II, some nights he **sleepwalks**: he walks off in a straight line, and if you follow him you find Herobrine
  waiting where he stops, looking at the ground. Steve digs there and finds something somebody buried. Meet Herobrine's
  eyes and he says "not yet". Herobrine is rare: at most one Herobrine moment a day before Act III.
- **Sanity** replaces memory stability. It is never on screen; you read it from Steve. Steady, he is friendly and curious.
  Uneasy, he counts, repeats himself and wants the lights on. Fraying, he talks to someone who isn't there, sees him where
  there's nothing and sometimes calls you by someone else's name. Breaking, Herobrine's words come out of his mouth.
  At nothing he is **Taken** for a while and doesn't know anyone. Daylight, torches and a player he trusts staying near him
  bring it back; the dark and deep caves wear it down.
- **Caves turn on you, slowly.** His torches from an older world already burning ahead of you. Mining inside the wall that
  stops when you stop. Footsteps one step behind yours. A torch behind you gone when you turn round. A sign deep down.
  And from Act II, someone standing at the far end of your light.
- **The wordless muttering and vowel whispers are gone.** Steve mutters real words now ("Not mine. Not mine."), and the
  whispers behind you say "turn around" and "I'm right behind you" in Herobrine's voice.
- **Eight new music pieces** in the style of the theme: the first dark of a night, deep underground, by the fire when
  Steve trusts you, when he is near, a flashback when a memory comes back, and a variation for each act card. They play
  through the game's own music player, on the Music slider.
- Rana was Steve's closest friend. Her lines now say so.

### Breaches you can watch

When Herobrine holds Steve's eyes, a memory breaks. Steve no longer disappears.

1. He **runs**, a long way, faster than you can sprint.
2. He stops about fifty blocks off and **falls apart where you can see it**: he sits with his hands over his head,
   paces, stares at something that isn't there, hits at nothing, walls himself in with dirt.
3. If you walk up to him and he doesn't trust you, he shouts and runs again. If he trusts you (60 or more) and you walk
   up slowly and stay next to him, he calms down. That is one of the most important memories in the journal.
4. After enough breaches he doesn't calm down at night. He goes to the home of the player he trusts most
   and takes it apart. In the morning he remembers nothing.

### Herobrine

- He has the **original Herobrine look**: Steve's own skin with blank white eyes that glow in the dark.
  (In 0.1 the game was drawing him as a plain player. That is fixed.)
- **He senses you.** Looking at him, standing near him and running near him all fill a hidden meter.
  Sneaking halves it. Your screen darkens at the edges and your heart gets louder as it fills.
- In the first two acts he only watches, and leaves when you notice him too much.
- From Act III, when the meter is full, **he hunts you**. He walks at first. Then he doesn't. He will follow you until you are
  dead, through walls if he has to. The death message is "was removed".
- The only way out is Steve: if a Steve who trusts you is close by, he steps in front of you. It costs him a breach.
- You can right-click Herobrine and type **one** question in chat. Don't ask a second one. Don't hit him.

### The removed ones

Three figures from a much older version of the world: **Rana**, **Black Steve** and **Beast Boy**.
They are see-through, they never move their legs, and they fade when you get close. They never hurt you.
Sometimes they leave something behind.

### Effects and sound

- Screen effects: dread vignette with a heartbeat pulse, static, a flash of white eyes, blackouts, camera shake.
- An ambience director that gets worse with each act: low drones, howling wind, knocking behind you when you are indoors,
  footsteps in the grass, cave sounds, and later whispers and breathing right behind your shoulder, bursts of static and eyes in the dark.
- Original sounds and music, made for this mod with `tools/make_sounds.py` and `tools/make_voice.py`,
  so there is nothing borrowed in the jar. Every sound effect has a subtitle.

## Settings (operators)

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/leftbehind settings hunt false` | Herobrine never hunts players |
| `/leftbehind settings creative_kill false` | A hunt can't kill players in creative mode |
| `/leftbehind settings griefing false` | Steve and the story never change blocks |

Block changes also respect the `mob_griefing` game rule.

## Building

You need JDK 25.

```
./gradlew build
```

The mod jar ends up in `build/libs/left-behind-<version>.jar`.

`./gradlew runClientGameTest` starts a real game, walks through the main features and saves screenshots to
`build/run/clientGameTest/screenshots`.

The sounds, skins and data files are generated by the scripts in `tools/` (Python with numpy, scipy, soundfile, Pillow, and ffmpeg).
Steve's voice is recorded by `tools/make_voice.py` with the [Kokoro-82M](https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro) voice model
(Apache-2.0), run locally; the script explains how to get the model. Run it again after changing what Steve says.
